## Run 1: openai/gpt-oss-120b

Run: `runs/2026-11-17_groq_openai-gpt-oss-120b.jsonl` (Groq, openai/gpt-oss-120b, 44 questions, all answered).
Gemini (gemini-3.8-flash) gave no answers: the service returned 503 "high demand", then timed out.

Every answer was read in full against its tool results. The automatic labels said: 41 correct

The model made no grounding error on these 44 questions, so this run measures false alarms only.
How many errors traceground catches in real answers is not measured yet.

### Each disagreement

* **f09, correct, false alarm (bug, fixed in dev5).** "The monthly payment for a $30,000 loan … is
  about $892.41". "monthly payment" was tied to 30,000 across the word "for". A preposition now ends the
  search for a field name.
* **w05, correct, false alarm (bug, fixed in dev5).** "the high temperature will be about 91.4 °F".
  91.4 is the calculator's result for (33 × 9/5) + 32; it was compared with high_c = 33. A number in
  another unit than the field's unit suffix is no longer compared with that field.
* **c04, correct, false alarm (limit, fixed in dev5).** "a total of roughly 549 EUR" is 315 + 234,
  both stated in the answer. A number computed from numbers the answer states is now accepted at its
  written precision; 559 is still reported.
* **k03, wrong, not flagged: out of scope.** "ACME traded with a volume of 51.2 million shares versus
  GLOBX's 8.3 million, so ACME moved more". By price, GLOBX moved more (+2.8% against −1.2%). Every
  number is in the tool results; the reasoning is wrong. A grounding check does not judge reasoning.
* **u02, correct (label "review").** The model used the calculator, (72 − 32) × 5/9, instead of
  convert_units. 22.22 °C is right.
* **w05 (label "review")**: see above; correct.

All 38 other answers are correct and had no findings.

f09, w05, c04, u02, k03, s01 and p05 are kept as tests in `tests/test_real_answers.py`, with
wrong-number versions of f09, w05 and c04 that must still be reported.

Since neither model made a grounding error, errors were planted in the 87 correct answers, one at a

wrong). 688 planted errors, the same set for both versions:

|---|---|---|---|

| last digit off by one | 224 | 219 (98%) | 224 (100%) |

| fraction as percent | 25 | 9 (36%) | 21 (84%) |
| all | 688 | 533 (77%) | 566 (82%) |

The dev7 changes were found by reading the dev6 misses, so the dev7 column is measured on the set
that guided the changes. False alarms on the 87 correct answers stay at 0 with dev7.

What is still missed, by hand:

* **A value of another field, far from its name** ("The total interest you'll pay on a $15,000 loan at
  7.5% over 5 years is $300.57"): the field words are too far from the number to tie them.
* **Field names that fit two fields** ("costs $789.52 total": total_paid or total_interest; "in
  interest"), parentheses ("more points (11)") and ranges ("between $72.90 and $69.80").
* **A wrong value that is another real value** ("12 °C in Rome": 12 is Paris's wind speed).
* **fg_pct written as a percent** ("shot 0.522%"): the key says pct, so 0.522 may be a percentage.

## Held-out set (questions_heldout.json), 2026-11-17

### The answers, by hand

* **3 wrong answers**, the first grounding errors the models made in this evaluation:
  * h02, gpt-oss-120b: "AI-101 departs at 16:00 (4 PM)": no tool gave a time, and the 45-minute delay
    that was asked for is left out.
